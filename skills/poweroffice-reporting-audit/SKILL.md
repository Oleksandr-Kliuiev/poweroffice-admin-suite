# PowerOffice Reporting and Audit

Keep the investigation read-only unless the user separately requests a correction. Verify company, organization number, report, accounting or payroll basis, date range, status filters, currency, dimensions, and whether figures are posted-only or include drafts.

Read [references/reporting-audit.md](references/reporting-audit.md) for report scoping, drilldown, reconciliation, complete-list handling, exports, privacy, and evidence quality.

Prefer stable report parameters and source-document drilldown over screenshots alone. Distinguish observations from conclusions and PowerOffice data from external bank, tax, or source-document evidence.

Minimize payroll and personal data. Do not export more rows or fields than needed, and never place sensitive exports in the public repository or company catalog.

Finish with company, report names, exact filters and basis, as-of time, totals, discrepancies, sampled/drilled source identifiers, export location when requested, limitations, and recommended next checks. Do not claim reconciliation when only one side was examined.
