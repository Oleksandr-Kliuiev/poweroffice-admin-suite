# PowerOffice Sales and Invoicing

Read the [Chrome contract](../poweroffice-admin-suite/references/browser-operation-contract.md) if absent from working context; retain it across related commands. Load only the relevant workflow section below, including its prerequisites and verification.

Use [invoicing operations](references/invoicing-operations.md): **Document states**, then **Create or edit a draft**, **Critical commit: send or bulk generate**, or **Credit note**. Resolve customer, product and invoice numbers; search existing drafts/recurring orders before creation.

Sending invoices/credits and bulk generation are critical commits. Include project/department and affected count alongside the reference's delivery/date/currency/VAT/attachment checks. Do not infer VAT exemptions, revenue accounts, delivery method, bank/OCR setup or customer email; verify catalog defaults live.

Report exact customer/document numbers, totals, persisted draft/confirmed/sent state, posting/delivery outcome, warnings and required human steps.
