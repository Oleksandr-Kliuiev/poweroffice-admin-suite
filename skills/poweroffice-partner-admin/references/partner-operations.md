# Partner and multi-client operations

## Resolve scope

Inventory the exact partner workspace, clients, organization numbers, users, existing direct/client roles, template roles, and special responsibilities. Clear filters and traverse the complete client list before claiming a user has no access elsewhere.

## Template-role change

1. Open the exact template role and capture current privileges and assignments.
2. Calculate the privilege delta, especially role/user administration, payments, payroll, accounting posting, invoices, reports, logs, and settings.
3. Use the built-in impact preview to record affected user and client counts.
4. Inspect inclusions, exclusions, protected clients, and exceptions.
5. Present the critical-commit summary and rollback plan before Publish.
6. After publication, verify the template and representative assignments across affected clients; record propagation still pending.

## Bulk assignment or removal

Resolve every user by exact email and every client by organization number. Search current assignments before adding. For removals or deactivation, identify Administrator, subscription-administrator, security-contact, payment, payroll, and approval responsibilities and transfer them first.

Never use a visible row count as proof of the complete selection. Traverse all pages or virtual rows and reconcile the selected count to the impact preview.

Official reference: [Template roles and multi-client role management](https://support.poweroffice.com/hc/no/articles/33904563900061-Effektiv-rollestyring-med-malroller).
