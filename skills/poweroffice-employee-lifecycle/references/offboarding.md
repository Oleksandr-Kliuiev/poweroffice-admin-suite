# Employee offboarding

## Preflight

Verify exact employee, email, employee number, last working day, employment end date, final payroll intent, outstanding time/expense/travel submissions, approval responsibilities, project ownership, and whether login access should end immediately or on a specified date.

Identify protected or special responsibilities: Administrator, subscription administrator, security contact, payment approver, payroll administrator, role administrator, employee approver, project manager, or integration owner. Reassign required responsibilities before deactivation.

## Safe workflow

1. Record current employee, user, role, approval, project, and payroll state.
2. Reassign approver and project responsibilities to exact named successors.
3. Review outstanding timesheets, expenses, travel claims, supplier approvals, and payments; do not approve financial items merely to clear a queue.
4. Set the verified employment end date and relevant employment status.
5. Prepare final-payroll requirements, but do not approve payroll or submit A-melding without its critical commit checkpoint.
6. Remove or deactivate user access at the requested time while preserving employee and accounting history.
7. Verify user status, remaining roles, employee end state, and successor ownership after refresh.

Never delete historical employee, payroll, invoice, voucher, or approval records as a shortcut. If PowerOffice blocks deactivation because of a special responsibility, report the exact responsibility and required successor.

## Completion

Report access status, employment end state, reassigned responsibilities, outstanding items, final-payroll readiness, critical commits not performed, and required human or statutory follow-up. Do not include salary, bank, tax, or identity-number values.
