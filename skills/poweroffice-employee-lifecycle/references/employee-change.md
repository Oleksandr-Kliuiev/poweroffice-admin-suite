# Employee change

Use this workflow for department, position, manager/approver, access role, working hours, project responsibility, or employment-term changes.

1. Establish the effective date and whether the change is prospective or corrects historical data.
2. Capture current employee, user, role, approver, project, and payroll-relevant state.
3. Resolve the destination department, role, approvers, activities, and projects by exact stable identifiers.
4. Apply only requested changes; do not copy all settings from a peer employee.
5. Keep historical accounting and payroll records intact. Do not backdate changes that alter submitted reporting without exact authorization and a correction plan.
6. Verify all changed surfaces after refresh and report effective dates and downstream payroll/time/reporting effects.

If the change would alter already approved payroll, submitted A-melding, paid expenses, posted invoices, or a locked accounting period, stop and route the correction to the appropriate specialist workflow.
